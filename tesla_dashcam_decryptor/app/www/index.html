F · Fix telemetry sync

A bug (fixed in 0.7.15) could make the telemetry HUD lag the video by up to 18 seconds on clips where the car's telemetry stream stopped partway through recording — the HUD kept showing the car still moving after it had visibly come to a stop. Existing clips keep their old, incorrectly-timed data until re-extracted from the mp4 already on disk. Nothing is deleted and no clip is re-decrypted.

G · Free up storage — delete clips (permanent)
